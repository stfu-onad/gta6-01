An open-world crime game built for the [make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

You are **Rex Marlowe**, fresh out of the marina lock-up and broke in **Neon Bay** — a sun-soaked port city of neon, fast cars and faster deals. Steal a ride, run jobs for the fixer **Vera Solano**, dodge the Bay Police, and take the crown of the bay.

## What is in the game

- **A living 3D city** (three.js, vendored): a road grid with sidewalks, procedural towers with lit windows and neon signs, street lamps, Coral Park with a fountain, a beach and the sea, all under a sunset sky
- **On foot and by car**: third-person walking with run and jump, plus drivable cars you can steal — sedans, sports cars, taxis, vans and trucks
- **Traffic and pedestrians**: NPC cars cruise the grid and turn at intersections; pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and run when shots ring out
- **Wanted level 0–5**: crimes raise the heat; Bay Police cars chase and ram you, officers chase you on foot and can bust you; lay low and the stars fade
- **A 5-mission story**: steal your first wheels, run a timed delivery, survive a 3-star heat, rob the corner store, then wreck Duke Kessler's gold coupe and take the crown — with cash rewards and mission markers on the world and the minimap
- **Combat**: fists up close, a pistol with raycast hits, muzzle flash, tracers, sparks and car explosions
- **HUD with a live minimap**: health, cash, wanted stars, clock, objective, speed, plus a start screen, pause menu, game-over screen and autosave in the browser
- **Synthesised sound**: engine hum, gunshots, sirens, explosions and a small sunset music loop — all made in code with WebAudio, no audio files

## Controls

WASD move · mouse look (click to lock) · SHIFT run · SPACE jump · E enter/exit car · click shoot or punch · 1 fists · 2 pistol · R reload · ESC pause · M mute

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server — for example `node serve.ts` — and open it in desktop Chrome. Everything is procedural and offline: no network requests.

## Entry

Built for the make.fun competition **gta6-01** ("Make GTA 6"). The game is entirely original: its city, characters, vehicles, story, art and music are all made for this entry — nothing from any commercial game.
